OUT NOW · 1 AUGUST 2026

Cable parking,
and a rack you can
edit with a finger.

This update is about the two things that a mouse gives you for free and a touchscreen does not: reaching a module that is off screen, and picking out the modules you want to work on.

🧵

Cable parking

A bar on the left edge holds a cable end while you scroll to its destination. It starts with three holes and grows to ten as you fill them, shows which module and port each end came from, and lights up every compatible jack while you aim. Drops now snap to the highlighted jack.

✅

Select by tapping

With multi-select on, a tap adds a module to the selection and a second tap removes it — knobs and ports stay put while you choose. A hold picks the whole selection up and moves it.

🔴

A halo, not a wash

Selected modules are ringed with a red glow instead of being covered by a red tint, so you can still read the panel you just selected.

🗑️

Delete from the toolbar

A bin button removes the selected modules. It always asks first and tells you how many are going — and undo is right next to it.

🪟

Sixteen tools, two rows

The toolbar now lays its tools out on a fixed eight-column grid: build and play on the first row, editing and locks on the second. Finger-sized on a phone, and it remembers whether you left it open.

🎓

A tour that fits the screen

The first-run tour measures the real interface instead of guessing, so its spotlights land on the toolbar, the palette and the cable bar on any screen size, in portrait and in landscape.

🛡️

Safer module packs

Installing a .rdmod now validates the archive thoroughly before any native code is loaded, and warns you that a pack runs with the app's permissions. Cloud backup and device transfer of app data are switched off.

🩹

Recovery start-up

If the app fails to start twice in a row, the third launch skips the saved session and side-loaded packs. Your autosave is moved aside, never deleted.
